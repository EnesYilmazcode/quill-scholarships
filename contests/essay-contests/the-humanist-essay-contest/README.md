# The Humanist Essay Contest

**Sponsor:** American Humanist Association (The Humanist magazine)  

**Prompt.** Humanist Essay Contest for Young Men and Women of North America (American Humanist Association / The Humanist magazine): an essay of 1,500 to 2,500 words dealing with humanist themes, open to students in grades 9-12 in the U.S. and Canada. Judged on originality of thought, sense of emotional engagement, clarity and quality of presentation, amount of research evidenced, and future potential shown by the author.
