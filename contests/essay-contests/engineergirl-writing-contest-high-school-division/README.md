# EngineerGirl Writing Contest (High School division)

**Sponsor:** National Academy of Engineering  

**Prompt.** Write a short essay in which you: Imagine how new innovations related to one of the 20 Greatest Engineering Achievements of the 20th Century will be different as more women and non-white engineers are involved in designing solutions. Use a specific example from your own experience or community to express the importance of different perspectives, and explain how diverse engineers can lead to better solutions. Identify how a technology related to the selected achievement may have affected one group of people differently than another, and speculate about how an engineer from this group might approach the problem differently than has been done in the past. Provide an example of how diverse engineering teams in the 21st century have created, or are working to create, better solutions to the same problems engineers \"solved\" in the last century. (High school essays must be no more than 750 words, with a reference list of 3-10 resources.)
