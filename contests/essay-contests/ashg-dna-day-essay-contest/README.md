# ASHG DNA Day Essay Contest

**Sponsor:** American Society of Human Genetics (ASHG)  

**Prompt.** Choose one genetic treatment or therapy that has been developed or widely used in recent years (e.g. Casgevy for sickle cell disease, Roctavian for hemophilia). Explain what the treatment is and how it works. Discuss its advantages and disadvantages, considering ethical issues, effects on society, cost, and accessibility. If you or someone you care about had this condition, would you choose this treatment? Explain why, considering age, overall health, cost factors, etc. (high school students)
