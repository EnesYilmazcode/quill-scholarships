# Elie Wiesel Prize in Ethics Essay Contest

**Sponsor:** The Elie Wiesel Foundation for Humanity  

**Prompt.** Elie Wiesel Prize in Ethics Essay Contest (college juniors and seniors): in 3,000 to 4,000 words, write a thought-provoking essay that raises questions, singles out issues and identifies ethical dilemmas on any subject related to ethics; the essay may take the form of an analysis that is biographical, historical, literary, philosophical, psychological, sociological or theological, with an individual voice evident. (Standing contest guidance; the Foundation's suggested topics vary by year.)
