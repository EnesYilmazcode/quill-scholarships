# The Fountain Essay Contest: Real or Fake? Reflections on How We Experience Life in the AI Age

**Sponsor:** The Fountain Magazine (Blue Dome Press)  

**Prompt.** Real or Fake? Reflections on How We Experience Life in the AI Age. New AI technologies have introduced amazing capabilities for content creation - beyond what we could have imagined. ... But do they feel as genuine as we would normally expect? Do we feel the same sense of fulfilment or claim ownership as we otherwise would? Immersed in our screens, how real (or fake) does life feel? Do our relationships matter at all? How real are our friendships with others? Participate in the contest and share your reflections. (Ideal word count 1,500 to 2,500.)
