# The Fountain Essay Contest: Where Is Home?

**Sponsor:** The Fountain Magazine (Blue Dome Press)  

**Prompt.** Where is home? \"Home\" holds a special place in our lives. A large, luxurious mansion or a small, decrepit place, our homes are hard to replace with anything. Our earliest memories form inside its walls; we utter our first words and take our first steps there. Does everyone feel the same about home? Is it where you were born or where you earn your bread? Is home a physical place? In a world that moves faster than ever and is confined to small screens, how do perceptions about \"home\" change? Where is home for people who are forced to leave their homes? Where is your home? (Ideal word count 1,500 to 2,500.)
