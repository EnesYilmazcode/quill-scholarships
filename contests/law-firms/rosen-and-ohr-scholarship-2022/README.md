# Rosen & Ohr Scholarship 2022

**Sponsor:** Rosen & Ohr, Florida  

**Prompt.** Write an essay explaining in detail how a serious accident involving you, a family member, or a close friend has impacted your life. This could have been a car crash, a slip-and-fall accident, a work-related injury, or any other serious accident. Explain how an injury from the accident has affected you or a loved one by: Limiting or restricting daily activities; Affecting feelings and relationships; Impacting finances.
